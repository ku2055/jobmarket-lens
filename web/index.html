Data Sources and Methodology:

* Data sources: job postings published on the official career sites of S&P 500 companies (constituents as of ; Berkshire Hathaway is excluded). Coverage by company is listed under Source Coverage above.

Postings come only from each company's official career site. Collectors call the site's own job API where one exists (Workday, Oracle Recruiting, SuccessFactors, Eightfold, Jibe, amazon.jobs) and otherwise read the schema.org JobPosting data on each posting page. Postings without a source date remain in inventory with an unknown date. Sites that refuse automated access are listed as blocked, not estimated.

Full descriptions, raw responses, extraction snippets and observation history are stored privately. This public preview contains only the limited posting metadata and derived measures needed for these charts.

This build starts its own local history; the author's private history is not included. All observed postings are retained, including undated postings. Original source dates are preserved. Undated and future-dated records are excluded from posting-date charts. First discovery is not evidence that a job was newly created.

Open means observed in the latest collection attempt for that employer. A posting absent from one verified complete collection is possibly closed; after two complete collections it is closed. Missing records in capped, failed, blocked or unverified collections do not establish closure. Closure does not imply a hire. Counts describe postings, not vacancy headcount.

Complete means pagination reached the reported total or an empty page, and the unique count matched the site's reported total. Other collections are partial or unverified. Pay with an inferred period, unknown currency, or suspicious range is excluded from pay aggregates and retained for review. Annualizing hourly pay assumes 2,080 hours, including for part-time roles.

Occupation groups follow the 2018 Standard Occupational Classification (SOC) major groups, assigned by auditable title rules with the company's own job family as a fallback. Pay is the posted range, annualized for comparison; it is not actual earnings. Skills and licenses are matched against a fixed dictionary, so a skill the dictionary lacks will not appear. "Not specified" means the collector or parser did not establish a value; it does not prove the employer omitted it.
